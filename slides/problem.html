Three sensors. None of them enough.

IMU

Acceleration

Drifts. Badly.

Altimeter

Height only

Blind sideways.

Optical flow

Sideways speed

Worse up high.

And the wind is invisible. It moved our drone 103 metres in sixty seconds.

They also disagree — and report at three different rates.

Each sensor is individually inadequate. The IMU gives acceleration, and integrating it twice turns a small bias into a huge position error — it drifted over a hundred metres in our one-minute flight. The altimeter gives height and nothing about horizontal position. Optical flow gives horizontal velocity but degrades with altitude, because there are fewer ground features to track — remember that detail, it comes back later as the error we caught. On top of all that, the wind is never measured by anything, and it is the dominant disturbance. They also run at three different rates, so they cannot simply be lined up and compared. Our job is to fuse three partial, noisy, differently-timed signals into one consistent estimate, and to recover the wind as a bonus.
