Three sensors, none of them enough

100 Hz

IMU

Gives acceleration. Integrate it twice and small errors compound into metres within seconds.

10 Hz

Altimeter

Height only, ±0.6 m of noise. Says nothing about where the drone is horizontally.

30 Hz

Optical flow

Horizontal velocity, and it gets worse the higher you fly — fewer ground features to track.

And the wind is invisible. No sensor measures it, yet it is the single biggest thing pushing the drone off course — in our data it moved the aircraft about 200 metres downwind in 60 seconds.

The estimator has to recover what no instrument reports.

Each sensor is individually inadequate. The IMU drifts because double integration compounds error. The altimeter only sees height. Optical flow only sees horizontal velocity, and it degrades with altitude — remember that detail, it comes back later as a bug we caught. Above all, the wind is never measured by anything, and it is the dominant disturbance. Our job is to fuse these three partial, noisy, differently-timed signals into one consistent estimate, and to recover the wind as a bonus.
