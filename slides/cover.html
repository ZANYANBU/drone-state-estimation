Estimating a drone’s position
and velocity in turbulent wind

Linear Dynamical Systems and Kalman filtering, applied to three noisy sensors that disagree — and a wind nobody measures.

Team Cash Guard

V. Anbu Chelvan  ·  S. Roopesh  ·  B. Charith Reddy  ·  Y. Rupesh Reddy

238× better than
integrating the IMU

Our problem: a drone flying in gusty wind has to know where it is and how fast it is going. It carries an IMU, an altimeter and an optical-flow camera. All three are noisy, they run at different rates, and none of them measures the wind that is pushing the drone around. We solve it with a Linear Dynamical System and a Kalman filter — which, as we will show, is the same machinery as the HMM material from this unit, moved from discrete states to continuous ones. The headline result is that the filter is 238 times more accurate than simply integrating the accelerometer.
