Started 3× wrong. EM recovered σalt = 0.593 against a true 0.600, log-likelihood rising every step.

We never told the filter how noisy the sensors were — it worked it out from the data.

Two more unit-4 algorithms, both delivering. The RTS smoother is the backward half of forward-backward. The filter is causal — at time t it only knows the past — but once the flight is over we know everything, so we sweep backwards and let later evidence improve earlier estimates. That is precisely gamma proportional to alpha times beta, and it cut our velocity error by a factor of three and a half. Then EM: we deliberately started the filter believing the sensors were three times noisier than they really are, and EM recovered the altimeter noise to within one per cent. The log-likelihood increased monotonically at every iteration, which is the theoretical guarantee of EM showing up in practice.
