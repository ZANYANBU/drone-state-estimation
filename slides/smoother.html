Two more unit-4 algorithms, both delivering. The RTS smoother is the backward half of forward-backward. The filter is causal - at any moment it only knows the past - but once the flight is over we know everything, so we sweep backwards and let later evidence improve earlier estimates. That is gamma proportional to alpha times beta, and it cut our velocity error by a factor of three and a half. Then EM: we deliberately started the filter believing the sensors were three times noisier than they really are, and it recovered the altimeter noise to within one per cent. The log-likelihood increased at every iteration, which is EM's theoretical guarantee showing up in practice. Worth stressing: nobody fed the filter the true noise values. It worked them out from the data alone.
