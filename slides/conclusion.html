Conclusions

What we showed

  • Unit 4’s algorithms work on continuous states.
  • Treating wind as an answer keeps it linear.
  • Three weak sensors beat one, by 238×.
  • We found what nothing measures.

Limits

  • Gentle flight only. Aerobatics need an EKF.
  • The IMU reports thrust, not drag.
  • Wind is the same everywhere on the drone.
  • The trajectory is simulated.

Being accurate and knowing it are different things.
Only a consistency test tells you which you have.

Team Cash Guard  ·  Anbu · Roopesh · Charith · Rupesh

To close. We set out to estimate a drone's position and velocity from three inadequate sensors in turbulent wind, and we did it with the Unit 4 toolkit moved from discrete states to continuous ones. The headline numbers are a 238 times improvement over dead reckoning and the recovery of wind, which nothing on the aircraft measures. We are explicit about the limits: linear drag and gentle flight, a simplified IMU channel, uniform wind, and a simulated trajectory driven by real measured noise and real measured gusts. If we had to keep one lesson it is that being accurate and knowing you are accurate are different properties. Our filter had an excellent error while being badly over-confident, and only the NIS consistency test revealed it. Thank you - happy to take questions.
