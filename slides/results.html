Here is the payoff. On the left, the true path and the Kalman estimate are indistinguishable across a two hundred metre arc, while the IMU-only track stays near the origin — it never sees the wind, so it has no idea the drone has been blown downwind. Position error drops from 103 metres to 43 centimetres, a factor of 238. The smoother tightens that further to 32 centimetres and cuts velocity error by more than three times. The result we are proudest of is the last box: we estimate the wind to about 2.1 metres per second, even though not one sensor on the aircraft measures wind. It is recovered purely from the discrepancy between what the dynamics predicted and what the sensors reported.
