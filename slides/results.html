Here is the payoff. On the left, the true path and the Kalman estimate are indistinguishable across a long wind-blown arc, while the IMU-only track stays near the start - it never sees the wind, so it has no idea the drone has been blown a hundred metres downwind. Position error drops from a hundred and three metres to forty-three centimetres, a factor of two hundred and thirty-eight. The smoother tightens that to thirty-two centimetres and cuts velocity error by more than three times. The result we are proudest of is the last box: we estimate the wind to about two metres per second even though not one sensor on the aircraft measures wind. It is recovered purely from the gap between what the physics predicted and what the sensors reported.
